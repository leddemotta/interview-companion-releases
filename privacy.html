Meeting Companion · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Meeting Companion is a desktop application for Windows that transcribes your calls and interviews, suggests answers during interviews and writes a summary when a call ends. It is built so that your data stays on your PC and is sent only to the services you connect with your own accounts. This page explains what the app collects, where it goes and how to delete it.

No account, no server of ours

Meeting Companion has no sign-up and no server of its own. We do not receive your audio, transcripts, summaries, resume, profile, API keys or usage statistics. The app contains no analytics or telemetry.

The only connection to us is the update check: the app periodically asks GitHub whether a newer version is published. GitHub sees the request as it would see any download, including your IP address.

What is stored on your PC

  • Settings and profile: your name, photo, headline, resumes and notes, kept in the app's data folder.
  • API keys: the keys you paste for each service, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI) so that only your Windows account can read them.
  • Calls: each call's transcript, the suggested answers, the summaries, the title and the people's names, saved in the call history. You can rename, edit or delete any call, and uninstalling the app removes all of its local data.
  • Microphone and call audio: captured only while a call is running. Audio is streamed for transcription and is not saved to disk.

Services the app sends data to

Everything below is sent directly from your PC to the service, with the API key or account you provide. Each service's own privacy policy and data-retention rules apply to what it receives.

  • Deepgram (speech-to-text): the audio of your calls, streamed while a call runs, together with the list of terms you asked the app to recognise.
  • AssemblyAI (speech-to-text, optional): the audio of discussion circles, when you add an AssemblyAI key.
  • The AI provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or DeepSeek): the transcribed questions and conversation, your profile and resume, the job description and your notes, to suggest answers, write summaries and name calls.
  • ElevenLabs (text-to-speech, optional): the text of the interviewer's questions in practice sessions, to read them aloud with a natural voice.
  • Google Drive (optional): see below.

Google Drive

If you connect a Google account under Settings → Integrations → Cloud, the app keeps a copy of each call (transcript, summaries and details) in a folder named "Meeting Companion" in your Google Drive, so your calls survive this PC and can be imported on another one.

  • The app requests only the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it created itself, never the rest of your Drive.
  • The app stores a refresh token on your PC, encrypted like the API keys. It does not store your Google password.
  • A call deleted in the app is also deleted from the Drive folder. Disconnecting the account removes the token from your PC; the folder stays in your Drive until you delete it.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Meeting Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data received from Google is used only to keep the copy of your calls in your Drive; it is not sold, not used for advertising and not read by people.

Your choices

  • Listening starts and stops only when you say so, and you can mute the microphone at any time.
  • Every service is optional and works only with a key or account you add; you can remove a key or disconnect an account at any time under Settings → Integrations.
  • Calls can be deleted one by one from the call history. Settings → About → Delete all my data removes the settings, keys, profile and every call from this PC.

Children

Meeting Companion is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
