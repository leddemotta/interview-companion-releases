Meeting Companion

A Windows app that listens to your calls and interviews, shows the transcript live, suggests answers during interviews and writes a summary when the call ends.

Download for Windows

How it works

  • Bring your own keys: transcription by Deepgram (or AssemblyAI), answers and summaries by the AI you choose (Claude, GPT, Gemini or DeepSeek). Each service bills your own account.
  • Your data stays with you: calls, profile and keys live on your PC, keys encrypted. The app has no account and no server of its own.
  • Optional Google Drive copy: connect a Google account and every call is kept in a "Meeting Companion" folder in your Drive.
  • Interface in English, Portuguese and Spanish; calls in any language Deepgram supports.

Updates

Installed copies update themselves from the releases page, where every version and its notes are listed.
